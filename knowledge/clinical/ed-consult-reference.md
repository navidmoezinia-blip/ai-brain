# ED to FM admission consult reference (UTMB Galveston)
Purpose: phone-consult checklist plus the top 15 ED-to-FM admit complaints: what exam, labs and imaging to ask ED for, and the admit thresholds.
Source: Claude memory (hosp 2 project), 2026-11-07. Verify thresholds against UTMB protocol before clinical use.

## Phone consult checklist
1. Confirm the ask: admit / recs only / urgent bedside eval
2. Core data: age/sex, CC, vitals (current + trend), working dx, key exam findings, key labs/imaging (have them read the numbers), what's pending
3. Trajectory: time in ED, response to treatment, any acute change
4. Disposition-relevant: code status, baseline functional/cognitive status, social situation
5. Close the loop: callback number, bed request status, "anything else that made you call me"

## Admission-worthiness questions
1. Why can't this go home? What failed outpatient mgmt, what's the actual safety concern
2. Trajectory: improving/static/worsening on ED tx; resolves in obs window or needs >24h
3. Risk stratification: red-flag vitals/labs/trends, objective scores (PSI/CURB-65, Wells, HEART)
4. Can it be done outpatient safely? Needs IV meds/tele/serial exams? Rapid f/u path?
5. Patient-specific drivers: comorbidities lowering threshold, reliability/access/transport, recent bounce-back
6. Test ED's confidence: "biggest worry if sent home tonight?", attending already on board with admit?

## Top 15 complaints (Exam / Labs / Imaging / Admit if)

### 1. Chest pain / ACS r/o
- Exam: hypotension, new murmur, rales, unequal pulses/BP (dissection), reproducible?
- Labs: hs-trop (ask assay, initial value, delta on repeat); HEART components
- Imaging: EKG vs old (STE, new LBBB, TWI, STD), CXR (mediastinum, PTX)
- Admit: HEART >=4, any trop rise, dynamic EKG, unstable

### 2. CHF exacerbation
- Exam: JVD, S3, rales, edema, weight vs dry wt
- Labs: BNP >400 (or NT-proBNP age cut; compare to pt baseline, CKD runs high), trop, Cr/K/Na
- Imaging: CXR edema/effusion, last echo EF
- Admit: new O2 req, poor response to IV diuretic, trop+, hypotension, AKI

### 3. COPD / asthma exacerbation
- Exam: RR, accessory muscles, full sentences?, wheeze vs silent chest, sat vs baseline
- Labs: VBG/ABG (pCO2 >45 + pH <7.35 = BiPAP/admit); peak flow <50% pred (asthma)
- Imaging: CXR PNA/PTX
- Admit: hypercapnia, new O2 req, no improvement after 2-3 nebs + steroids, prior intubation

### 4. CAP
- Exam: confusion, RR >=30, SBP <90/DBP <=60, sat <90
- Labs: BUN >19, lactate, WBC; CURB-65 >=2 admit; MRSA/pseudomonas risk (prior isolate, hosp/IV abx <=90d)
- Imaging: CXR (multilobar, effusion)
- Admit: CURB-65 >=2, new O2 req, failed PO abx, can't take PO

### 5. Sepsis / UTI-pyelo
- Exam: qSOFA, MAP <65, CVA tenderness, Foley?
- Labs: lactate >2 (>4 = ICU), UA pyuria + nitrite, cultures before abx, Cr
- Imaging: renal US/CT if stone, obstruction, or not improving (hydro = urology emergency)
- Admit: lactate >2, hypotension post-fluids, obstruction, can't take PO, immunocompromised

### 6. Cellulitis / diabetic foot / abscess
- Exam: SIRS, borders marked?, crepitus/bullae/pain out of proportion (nec fasc: surgery now), probe-to-bone, pulses/ABI, abscess drained?
- Labs: WBC, CRP/ESR, glucose, lactate
- Imaging: XR (gas, osteo), MRI if osteo concern
- Admit: SIRS, failed PO abx, DFI with probe-to-bone/ischemia, needs IV/surgical debridement

### 7. DKA / HHS
- Exam: mental status, volume, Kussmaul
- Labs: glucose >250, pH <7.3, HCO3 <18, AG >12, ketones; K before insulin; Cr; HHS = glucose >600, osm >320
- Imaging: workup for precipitant (CXR, EKG for MI, cultures)
- Admit: all DKA; insulin drip = stepdown/ICU per UTMB; ask what precipitated

### 8. AKI / hyperkalemia
- Exam: volume status, bladder scan/retention
- Labs: Cr vs baseline, UOP, K >5.5 + EKG changes (peaked T, wide QRS) = urgent; meds (ACEi/NSAID/diuretic)
- Imaging: renal US if obstruction suspected
- Admit: K >6 or EKG changes, Cr >2x baseline, oliguria, AEIOU dialysis indications

### 9. Hyponatremia
- Exam: mental status, seizure, volume status
- Labs: Na, serum osm, urine osm, urine Na, TSH/cortisol if unclear; meds (thiazide, SSRI)
- Admit: Na <120, or <125 symptomatic, or acute drop; correction goal <=8/24h

### 10. GI bleed
- Exam: HR, orthostatics, melena vs hematochezia, cirrhosis stigmata
- Labs: Hgb + delta from baseline, BUN/Cr >30 (upper source), INR/plt, anticoagulants, type & screen
- Admit: Glasgow-Blatchford >=2 (0-1: discharge), hemodynamic change, cirrhosis, anticoagulated; GI aware?

### 11. Alcohol withdrawal
- Exam: tremor, HR, diaphoresis, CIWA score, hallucinations
- Labs: EtOH level, glucose, Mg/phos/K, LFTs/lipase/ammonia; PAWSS
- Hx: last drink time, prior DTs/seizures, benzos already given, thiamine given
- Admit: CIWA >=10 needing repeat benzos, hx DTs/seizure, comorbidity, no safe outpatient option

### 12. Syncope
- Exam: orthostatics, murmur, neuro deficit
- Labs: EKG (QT, WPW, Brugada, block, ischemia), trop, Hgb, glucose, BNP
- Hx: exertional?, no prodrome, family hx SCD, structural heart dz
- Admit: any positive San Francisco rule item (CHF, Hct <30, abnl EKG, SOB, SBP <90), or exertional/cardiac features

### 13. AMS / delirium (elderly)
- Exam: focal deficit (stroke path), baseline per family, meds (anticholinergic/benzo/opioid)
- Labs: glucose, Na, Ca, ammonia, UA, TSH, tox/EtOH, cultures if febrile
- Imaging: CT head (esp. fall or anticoag)
- Admit: no reversible cause found, unsafe gap from baseline, new focal finding

### 14. Pancreatitis
- Exam: pain, SIRS, PO tolerance
- Labs: lipase >3x ULN, TG, Ca, BUN >25, Hct; BISAP (BUN >25, AMS, SIRS, age >60, effusion)
- Imaging: RUQ US (gallstones); CT not needed early
- Admit: essentially all; ask severity (BISAP >=3, lactate, AKI = higher level)

### 15. PE / DVT
- Exam: HR, sat, BP, RV strain signs
- Labs: Wells/PERC, D-dimer, trop/BNP (RV strain: higher acuity), Cr for anticoag dosing
- Imaging: CTPA (clot burden, saddle, RV/LV ratio); LE doppler
- Admit: sPESI >=1, trop/BNP+, RV strain, O2 req, bleeding risk, unreliable f/u; sPESI 0 = outpatient candidate

### Bonus: HTN
- Urgency (no end-organ damage): discharge with f/u. Emergency (BP + trop/Cr/neuro/CXR edema/fundus): admit/ICU.
