## Section 2: Pre-Rounding Chart-Check Flow (What Info to Gather)

<details open>
<summary><strong>5-Minute Epic Pre-Rounding Sequence & Dot-Phrase Blind Spots</strong></summary>

| Step | Epic Tab / Source | Exact Anchors to Pull for Your Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Overnight Pass** | Sign-out, Rapid/Transfer notes, Nursing notes, Bedside procedure notes | Acute decompensations, floor BP lows, completed scopes/cath, nursing red flags (e.g., cough reflex, agitation), BMs/bleeding, family wishes |
| **2. Live MAR & Meds** | Continuous Infusions + Scheduled Meds | **Actual running pressor/inotrope/sedation/diuretic drips & rates**, antibiotic start + **`EOT` dates**, steroids, insulin regimen, held home BP/anticoagulant meds + why |
| **3. Flowsheets & Avatar** | Vitals, Resp/Vent, I/O, Lines Avatar | 24h `Tmax`, `MAP` lows, net 24h I/O + hourly `UOP`, Vent/BiPAP/HFNC settings & `SBT` pass/fail, **arterial lines / midlines / PICCs / dialysis caths / feeding tubes** |
| **4. Lab & Gas Trends** | Results Review | **Baseline Cr vs. today's Cr**, `K`/`Mg`, `Hgb`/`Plt` trajectory, `WBC`/`ANC`, `LFTs`/`lactate`, latest `ABG`/`VBG` (`pH / pCO2 / pO2`) |
| **5. Micro, Imaging, Cath** | Micro, Radiology, Cardiology (`TTE` / `RHC`), Consult Notes | Culture collection dates + hours negative (`ng 24h`), positive organisms, viral/urinary antigens, `CXR`/`CT` reads, `TTE` (`EF %` + RV), `RHC` (`RA/PA/PCWP, CI`), specialist recs |
| **6. Bundle & Dispo** | Orders (`Code Status`, Diet, PPX), Case Mgmt notes | Active Code Status sync, `GI PPX`, `VTE PPX` (or hold reason), `MPOA` name/relationship, LTACH/facility insurance status |

> [!WARNING]
> **Three Dot-Phrase Traps to Check Manually Every Morning:**
> 1. **`Pressors (last 24 hours): None`**: Prints `None` whenever a continuous drip bag (e.g., `vasopressin 0.03`, `milrinone`, `bumex gtt`) hasn't had a rate/bag action in the lookback window. Always check the live MAR.
> 2. **Missing Devices in `Lines:`**: Arterial lines, midlines, wounds, and Dobhoff/NG tubes frequently drop out of `Objective -> Lines:`. Check the Lines Avatar.
> 3. **Missing Cath Reports in `Imaging:`**: Right Heart Cath (`RHC`) hemodynamic notes do not auto-pull into 48h Radiology impressions—paste `RHC` pressures, `CI`, impression, and plan manually in CCU notes.

## Section 3: Slot-by-Slot Writing Guide, Info Needed, & Example Variations

<details open>
<summary><strong>3A. Top Sections (`Reason for Admission`, `Major Events`, `Subjective`, `Objective` Overrides)</strong></summary>

* **`Reason for ICU admission:`**
  * *Instructions*: 1 acronym to 10-word noun phrase; no period.
  * *Variations*: Single transfer-trigger acronym (`SOB`, `AMS`) vs. syndromic sequence (`PE s/p Cardiac Arrest and Shock`; `Syncope, AICD shocks, c/f mixed septic and cardiogenic shock`). Optional manual lines: `Intubation Day: X` (for OSH intubations) or `Intubation Date: N/A`.
* **`Major Events in Last 24 Hours:`**
  * *Instructions*: 2–4 hyphen bullets (`- ...`), no periods. Include only escalations/transfers, pressor/antiarrhythmic shifts or inability to wean, completed scopes/cath, acute lab drops + workup (`Platelets dropped to 52, HIT ab and Hemolysis work up`), new positive cultures, or major LTACH/insurance hurdles.
  * *Variations*: Placed **above `Subjective`** in all MICU notes (kept below `Subjective` in CCU); headers vary (`Major Events in Last 24 Hours:` vs. `Last 24 hour events (major events):` vs. `24 Hour Events:`); bullet spacing `- Text` vs. tight `-Text`.
* **`Subjective`** (1–4 short present-tense sentences, strictly matched to patient state):
  1. *Intubated & deeply sedated*: `Patient is intubated and sedated`
  2. *Trached & lightly awake*: `Patient remains intubated via tracheostomy and sedated. Appears comfortable, in no acute distress. Patient with eyes open, responding to some questions.`
  3. *Non-intubated, obtunded*: `Patient is somnolent. Does not respond to commands.`
  4. *Awake & verbal*: `Patient reports feeling well this morning. She does not have SOB. She has been eating well. She reports no loose stools.`
* **Manual Overrides in `Objective`** (only when overriding SmartBlocks):
  * `Spontaneous Breathing Trial:`: Blank, omitted if non-intubated, or `SAT/SBT in AM`.
  * `Physical Exam:`: Point-and-click SmartBlock + short `Comments: On BIPAP` / `A&O x0` **OR** 8-line semicolon shorthand (`Constitutional`, `HEENT`, `Resp`, `Cardio`, `GI`, `MSK`, `Integ`, `Neuro`).

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>3B. Organ-System Writing Guide & Variations (`Assessment & Plan`)</strong></summary>

#### Global Depth Variations Across Any Organ System
1. **Active ICU System (3-Tier)**: `Header` -> `Stacked Diagnoses` -> `4-Beat Narrative Paragraph` -> `- Action Bullets`.
2. **Stable Chronic System (2-Tier)**: `Header` -> `Stacked Diagnoses` -> `- Action Bullets` (no narrative paragraph).
3. **Inactive System**: Omit header completely (e.g., baseline `Neuro` or normal `Heme`) **OR** write `No acute concerns` under `Renal, fluid, and electrolytes`.

#### System-by-System Info Needed & Variations
* **`Neuro` / `Neuro/Psych`**:
  * *Info*: `A&O` status, `RASS` target, encephalopathy/agitation cause (CO2 narcosis, metabolic, dementia, meth/EtOH withdrawal + `UDS`), `CTH` result, active sedation drip (`Precedex`, `Propofol/Fentanyl`), held neuroleptics (`Seroquel`, `lactulose`) + family wishes, `PT/OT`.
  * *Variations*: Omitted if baseline; 2-line `-PT/OT` stub; withdrawal watch; or full BiPAP-agitation narrative with indented sub-bullet.
* **`Pulmonology` / `Resp` / `Respiratory`**:
  * *Info*: Hypoxic vs. hypercapnic failure + etiology (edema, pneumonia, PE + `PESI`, viral), home O2 baseline, intubation/BiPAP trigger (`WOB`, `ABG`), `CXR`/`CT` & pleural pockets, `SBT` trajectory or `Tracheostomy` date, vent/BiPAP/HFNC settings (`O2 pp`), `SAT/SBT` or `PS trials`, `duoneb`/`hypersal`, respiratory antivirals/steroids (`EOT` date), `ABG`/`VBG` plan.
  * *Variations*: Placed 1st (`Pulmonology`) with pasted `Order info:` vent block vs. placed 2nd (`Resp`/`Respiratory`) with standard `-` vent/O2 bullets.
* **`Cardiovascular`**:
  * *Info*: Shock differential + `SCAI` stage, baseline BP, fluid/albumin boluses, pressor/inotrope arc (dates weaned off + current rates & `MAP goal >60`), `EF %` + `ICM`/NICM, `TTE`/`RHC` numbers (`CI`, `PCWP`), `CAD` (`CABG`/`PCI` + dates), arrhythmias/device settings, anticoagulation (`heparin gtt` vs `Eliquis`), diuretics + `Strict I&O`/fluid restriction, held vs. continued home cardiac meds, `Tele`, `K >4` (or `>4.5`), `Mg >2`.
  * *Variations*: Concise 2-sentence post-rapid or post-RHC update vs. longitudinal 10-sentence shock/VT/device summary; full dose bullets vs. bare chronic med lists (`-Lipitor`, `-Plavix`).
* **`FEN/GI` / `GI`**:
  * *Info*: SBO/gastritis/bleed check (`OGT` output `< 450 cc`, melena/BRBPR, `EGD`/ENT scopes), `Shock liver`/`LFT` trend, `CTAP` masses/`KUB`/`small bowel f/t`, `PEG`/`NG`/`Dobhoff` status, feed rate + free water flushes (`fwf 350ml q4h`), `PPI`, bowel regimen/`BMs`, `GI`/`Surgery`/`SLP`.
  * *Variations*: Full narrative + 6 bullets for active SBO/bleed/shock liver vs. 2-bullet no-narrative stub for chronic dysphagia/PEG.
* **`ID`** *(Always placed right after `FEN/GI`)*:
  * *Info*: `Tmax`, `WBC`/`ANC` (note steroid leukocytosis), source ranking, **full positive + negative micro/antigen ledger** (blood cx w/ `ng 24h` or contaminant `CoNS`, sputum cx, UA/UCx, viral PCR, `Legionella`/`Strep` Ag, `C. diff`, prior `ESBL` hx), completed abx w/ dates, active abx w/ **`EOT M/D`**, pending cx, chest tube/source control contingency.
  * *Variations*: 1-sentence viral summary + `EOT` bullet vs. full negative-workup paragraph justifying broad empiric coverage (`Vanc, cefepime` / `Merrem`).
* **`Renal, fluid, and electrolytes` / `Renal/Lytes` / `Renal`**:
  * *Info*: **`Baseline Cr X now Y`**, AKI status (`resolved`, `on CKD3`, `2/2 ATN`), `HAGMA`/`Hyponatremia`, `CRRT` or Lasix challenge response, `Monitor BMP and UOP`, `Bladder scan/straight cath if retaining`.
  * *Variations*: Full AKI/CRRT narrative vs. 2-line `- resolved` stack vs. `No acute concerns`.
* **`Endo`**:
  * *Info*: `T2DM` (`A1c` + date) / steroid hyperglycemia, basal + prandial + `SSI` insulin, thyroid/adrenal meds (`levothyroxine`, `stress dose steroids`, or `methimazole`/`prednisone`), `gout` (`allopurinol`).
  * *Variations*: Almost always a 1–2 bullet no-narrative block unless active Endocrine consult (e.g., amiodarone thyrotoxicosis).
* **`Heme/Onc` / `Heme` / `Heme/Onc/Other`**:
  * *Info*: Anemia (`AoCD`), thrombocytopenia count, pancytopenia/`CLL`, oozing sites, `DIC`/`HIT-ab`/`hemolysis` labs, `Monitor CBC`, **explicit transfusion trigger (`Hgb <7` or `<8` w/ CAD)**, **Lovenox/heparin hold count & restart rule**.
  * *Variations*: Complex heme-onc narrative merged with `Other` vs. 1-line stable anemia stub vs. active oozing/HIT-negative block vs. omitted.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>3C. Bottom Checklist, `HOSPITAL COURSE`, & Attending Attestation Variations</strong></summary>

* **Four Footer Variations**:
  1. *Standard MICU (`ICU Bundle:` / `ICU Issues:`)*: `Nutrition`, `VTE/DVT Prophylaxis`, `GI Prophylaxis`, `Code Status`, `MPOA/surrogate decision maker`, detailed LTACH/insurance `Dispo`.
  2. *Compact `Other:` Block*: `Stress ulcer prophylaxis`, `DVT prophylaxis`, manual `Lines: CVC, PIV x3, A-line (date)`, `Nutrition`.
  3. *`CCU CHECKLIST:` (ALL CAPS)*: `CODE`, `VENT`, `FEEDING/FLUIDS`, `ANALGESIA`, `SEDATION`, `THROMBOPROPHYLAXIS`, `ULCER (STRESS) PPX`, `GLYCEMIC CONTROL`, `SBT/SEDATION VACATION`.
  4. *Minimal Footer*: BiPAP flowsheet pull + `Prognosis: Guarded`.
* **`HOSPITAL COURSE:` (Rendered Below Signature Divide)**:
  * *Opening Formula*: `[Full Name] is a [Age]-year-old [male/female] with PMH of [conditions w/ EF %, CABG/PCI/device years] who presented with [symptoms & shock/respiratory state].`
  * *Two-Register Body*: First ~70% is polished, formal past-tense medical narrative; final ~30% is a choppy, date-stamped daily append log (`Weaned pressors off on M/D. ... On M/D, furosemide diuresis was initiated.`).

</details>

## Section 4: Visual Formatting, Whitespace, & Punctuation Rules

<details>
<summary><strong>Exact Spacing, Diagnosis Stacking, Bullet Mechanics, & Annotated Example</strong></summary>

### Whitespace & Line-Break Rules
* **Zero blank lines inside an organ system**: `System Header` -> `Diagnosis Stack` -> `Narrative Paragraph` -> `- Action Bullets` sit flush on consecutive lines.
* **1 blank line** separates one organ system from the next; **1–2 blank lines** separate major SOAP sections.
* **Horizontal divider** (`______________________________________________` or `-------------------------------------------------------`) separates the resident signature from `HOSPITAL COURSE:`.

### Annotated Organ-System Block
```text
Cardiovascular                                                     <-- Title Case, no colon
Undifferentiated shock, likely mixed cardiogenic vs septic         <-- No '#', no bullet, Sentence case, no period
HFrEF (EF 30-35%, ICM) s/p CRT-D                                   <-- Chronic background w/ parenthetical specs
Presents with syncope and multiple ICD shocks. TTE with EF         <-- Immediate next line (0 blank lines); pro-drop + "with"
20-25% w G2DD. Per cardiology, ischemia eval not indicated.        <-- Specialist attribution
Off pressors since M/D. Furosemide initiated on M/D.            <-- Dated trajectory milestones
- Continue midodrine 10 mg enteral TID                             <-- Immediate next line; '- ' prefix, no trailing period
- cards recs                                                       <-- Lowercase shorthand mixed freely with capitalized bullets
- K >4.5, Mg >2                                                    <-- Tight inequality ('>4.5')
- continue stress dose steroids                                    <-- Parent bullet
               - Biopsy deferred until patient stable              <-- Sub-bullet: 12-15 leading spaces + '- '
```

### Punctuation & Numeric Rules
* **Bullets**: Always ASCII `-` (`- ` standard; `-Text` tight variant in CCU). Omit periods on single-sentence bullets (~98%). Use **semicolons (`;`)** to pair an order + titration rule (`- HFNC; wean as tolerated`) and **commas (`,`)** for inline lab reasons (`- holding lovenox with plt 58, will resume once improved`).
* **Dates**: Current stay = `M/D` (`Off pressors since M/D`); recent test anchors = `M/D/YY` (`M/D/YY`); PMH = `(YYYY)` or `M/YYYY` (`CABG (YYYY)`, `M/YYYY`).
* **Drips & Units**: Standard ICU drips frequently omit units (`Bumex gtt at 1`, `milrinone 0.375`, `vasopressin 0.03`, `BiPAP: 20/5`). Inequalities attach tightly (`K >4.5`, `Hgb <8`, `MAP goal >60`).

</details>

## Section 5: Clinical Voice, Elliptical Grammar, & Shorthand Lexicon

<details>
<summary><strong>Telegraphic ICU Grammar Rules & Context-Dependent Abbreviations</strong></summary>

### Six Core Voice Rules
1. **Zero First-Person (`I` / `We`)**: Resident notes never use `"I"` or `"we"`. Write subjectless **`"Will [verb]..."`** (`Will repeat blood cultures.`, `Will get KUB.`). First-person `"I"` is reserved strictly for Attending Attestations.
2. **Subject-Dropping (Pro-Drop)**: Omit `"Patient"` / `"He"` / `"She"` (`Appears comfortable.`, `Presents with syncope...`, `Remains profoundly neutropenic.`, `Stopped dobutamine. Weaned off levo, on vaso.`).
3. **`"with"` (`w/`) Replaces Verbs (`showed` / `has`)**: `TTE with EF 20-25% w G2DD.`, `CXR with volume overload.`, `UA with pyuria.`
4. **Copula-Dropping (Omit `is` / `was` / `were`)**: `CTH unremarkable.`, `Blood and urine cultures negative.`, `Hemoglobin stable.`, `Pressor support off.`
5. **Article-Dropping (Omit `the` / `a` / `an`)**: `At trickle feed goal rate.`, `Placed on BiPAP in MICU.`
6. **Four Distinct Prose Registers**: `Subjective` (5–10w blunt present tense) -> `A&P Narrative` (6–16w clipped telegraphic sentences + `"Will..."` bridge) -> `Hospital Course` top 70% (18–35w formal past-tense prose) -> `Hospital Course` bottom 30% (3–12w dated daily log fragments).

### High-Yield Shorthand Disambiguation
| Shorthand | Meaning | Usage Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`C/w` vs `c/w`** | **Continue with** vs **consistent with** | Bullet start (`- C/w Merrem`) = *Continue with*. Prose (`CT chest c/w pna`) = *consistent with*. |
| **`c/f`** | Concern for | Problem titles (`C/f septic shock`) and prose (`c/f superimposed infection`). |
| **`2/2` / `2/2 above`** | Secondary to | `2/2 above` points directly to the diagnosis line immediately above it. |
| **`EOT <date>`** | End of Therapy date | Standard antimicrobial stop date (`remdesivir 100mg daily EOT M/D`). |
| **`O2 pp`** | Oxygen per protocol | Standard RT titration bullet (`- O2 pp`). |
| **`CTM`** | Continue to monitor | Standalone watchful-waiting bullet (`- CTM`). |
| **`fwf`** | Free water flushes | Enteral hydration bullet (`- fwf 350ml q4h`). |
| **`ng 24h`** | No growth at 24 hours | Pending blood culture status (`- f/u blood cultures M/D, ng 24h`). |
| **`AoCD` / `Aox0`** | Anemia of chronic disease / Alert & oriented x0 | Standard Heme and Neuro shorthand. |

</details>

## Section 6: Day 2+ Copy-Forward Hygiene & Auto-Populated Dot-Phrases

<details>
<summary><strong>5-Point Pre-Sign Scrub List & Catalog of Auto-Populated Fields to Ignore</strong></summary>

### 5-Point Copy-Forward Scrub List
1. **Cross-System Med Purge**: When stopping an antibiotic or drip in one section (e.g., `meropenem completed M/D` in `ID`), delete stale `"still on merrem"` or `"on levo"` text in `Heme`, `Resp`, or `Cardio`.
2. **Replace Relative Dates**: Convert `"today"`, `"yesterday"`, and `"this morning"` into explicit `M/D` dates (`on M/D`) so copy-forwarded prose stays accurate tomorrow.
3. **Rotate `Major Events`**: Roll yesterday's 24h bullets down into the relevant organ-system narrative and `HOSPITAL COURSE` tail, then write fresh 24h bullets.
4. **Sync `Code Status`**: Ensure manual footer `Code Status` matches the live top order (`Full` vs `DNR`).
5. **Refresh Hold Thresholds**: Update inline lab numbers inside held-med bullets (`now plt 58`, `Baseline Cr 1.8 now 2.18`).

### Auto-Populated Epic Fields (Handled by Dot-Phrases — Do Not Generate)
* **Header**: `Date of Service`, `ICU Day`, top `Code Status`.
* **Objective Tables**: `Ventilator Settings` & airway LDA days, `Sedation/Analgesia/Paralytics (last 24 hours)` MAR table, `Pressors (last 24 hours)` table, `Lines`, `Vital Signs`, `Intake/Output Summary (Last 24 hours)`, `Labs (last 24 hours)` & `Labs (last 48 hours)`, `Microbiology` table, `Imaging` 48h impressions.
* **SmartLists / Vanishing UI Links**: `{MICU SBT:30032053}`, `{UTMB_IP_RASS_VALUES:10166}`, `{STRESS ULCER PROPHYLAXIS:32045}`, `{DVT PROPHYLAXIS:32046}`, `{NUTRITION:32047}`, disappearing `{... :123}` reference/Hospital Course links, and deleted `{... :300000123}` DocuAid CDI blocks.

### 3. SECTION & LAYER FORMULAS
