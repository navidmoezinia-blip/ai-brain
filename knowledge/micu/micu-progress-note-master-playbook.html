▶ Section 2: Pre-Rounding Chart-Check Flow (What Info to Gather)
5-minute Epic sequence
Step Where in Epic Exact Anchors to Jot Down
1. Overnight Pass Sign-out, Rapid/Transfer, Nursing & Scope notes Floor BP lows, airway/BiPAP escalations, completed scopes/cath, nursing red flags (cough reflex, agitation), BMs/bleeding, family wishes
2. Live MAR & Meds Continuous Infusions + Scheduled Meds Actual running pressor/inotrope/sedation/diuretic rates, antibiotic start + EOT dates, steroids, insulin, held home BP/DOAC meds + why
3. Flowsheets & Avatar Vitals, Resp, I/O, Lines Avatar 24h Tmax, MAP lows, net 24h I/O + hourly UOP, Vent/BiPAP/HFNC & SBT pass/fail, A-lines, midlines, PICCs, dialysis caths, NG/OG/PEG tubes
4. Lab & Gas Trends Results Review Baseline Cr vs. today's Cr, K/Mg, Hgb/Plt trajectory, WBC/ANC, LFTs/lactate, latest ABG/VBG (pH / pCO2 / pO2)
5. Micro, Imaging, Cath Micro, Radiology, TTE/RHC, Consults Culture collection dates + hours negative (ng 24h), organisms, viral/urinary Ags, CXR/CT reads, TTE (EF % + RV), RHC (RA/PA/PCWP, CI), specialist recs
6. Bundle & Dispo Orders + Case Management Code Status sync, GI PPX, VTE PPX (or hold reason), MPOA name/relationship, LTACH/facility insurance appeal deadlines
Three Dot-Phrase Blind Spots to Check Manually:
1. Pressors table printing "None": Steady continuous bags (e.g., vasopressin 0.03, milrinone, bumex gtt) frequently do not appear in Pressors (last 24 hours) if no bag action fired. Always read the live MAR.
2. Missing access in "Lines:": Arterial lines, midlines, feeding tubes, and wounds routinely drop out of Objective -> Lines:. Check the Lines Avatar.
3. Missing RHC hemodynamics in "Imaging:": Cardiac cath procedure notes do not auto-pull into 48h Radiology impressions—paste RHC pressures, CI, impression, and plan manually in CCU notes.
▶ Section 3: Slot-by-Slot Guide, Info Needed & Example Variations
Top-to-bottom authoring rules

3A. Top Sections & Subjective

  • Reason for ICU admission: 1 acronym to 10-word noun phrase, no period. Variations: Single trigger acronym (SOB, AMS) vs. syndromic sequence (PE s/p Cardiac Arrest and Shock; Syncope, AICD shocks, c/f mixed septic and cardiogenic shock). Optional extra line: Intubation Day: 6 when intubated at an outside hospital.
  • Major Events in Last 24 Hours: 2–4 hyphen bullets (- ...), no periods. Placed above Subjective in all MICU notes (kept below Subjective in CCU). Header variants: Major Events in Last 24 Hours: | Last 24 hour events (major events): | 24 Hour Events:.
  • Subjective (4 State Templates):
    • Intubated & deeply sedated: Patient is intubated and sedated
    • Trached & lightly awake: Patient remains intubated via tracheostomy and sedated. Appears comfortable, in no acute distress. Patient with eyes open, responding to some questions.
    • Non-intubated, obtunded: Patient is somnolent. Does not respond to commands.
    • Awake & verbal: Patient reports feeling well this morning. She does not have SOB. She has been eating well. She reports no loose stools.
  • Optional Objective Overrides: Spontaneous Breathing Trial: SAT/SBT in AM; Physical Exam via SmartBlock + Comments: On BIPAP OR 8-line semicolon shorthand (GI: soft; non-tender; non-distended; normoactive bowel sounds).

3B. Global Organ-System Depth Variations

1. Active ICU System (3-Tier)
Header → Stacked Diagnoses → 4-Beat Narrative Paragraph → - Action Bullets (0 blank lines).
2. Stable Chronic System (2-Tier)
Header → Stacked Diagnoses → - Action Bullets (omits narrative paragraph; standard for routine Endo/GI).
3. Inactive System
Either omit system header (baseline Neuro / normal Heme) OR write No acute concerns under Renal.
Neuro / Neuro/Psych
Info Needed: A&O status, RASS goal, encephalopathy/agitation cause (CO2 narcosis, metabolic, dementia, meth/EtOH withdrawal + UDS), CTH result, active sedation drip (Precedex, Propofol/Fentanyl), held neuroleptics (Seroquel, lactulose) + family wishes, PT/OT.
Variations: Omitted if baseline; 2-line -PT/OT stub; withdrawal watch; or BiPAP-agitation narrative w/ indented sub-bullet.
Pulmonology / Resp / Respiratory
Info Needed: Hypoxic vs. hypercapnic failure + etiology (edema, pna, PE + PESI, viral), home O2, intubation/BiPAP trigger (WOB, ABG), CXR/CT & pleural pockets, SBT pass/fail or Trach date, vent/BiPAP/HFNC settings (O2 pp), SAT/SBT or PS trials, duoneb/hypersal, antivirals/steroids (EOT), ABG/VBG plan.
Variations: Placed 1st w/ pasted Order info: vent block vs. placed 2nd w/ standard - vent/O2 bullets.
Cardiovascular
Info Needed: Shock differential + SCAI stage, baseline BP, fluid/albumin response, pressor/inotrope arc (dates weaned off + current rates & MAP goal >60), EF % + ICM/NICM, TTE/RHC numbers (CI, PCWP), CAD (CABG/PCI + dates), arrhythmias/ICD settings, anticoagulation (heparin gtt vs Eliquis), diuretics + Strict I&O/fluid limit, held vs continued home meds, Tele, K >4 (or >4.5), Mg >2.
Variations: 2-sentence post-rapid/post-RHC update vs. 10-sentence shock/VT/device summary; full dose bullets vs. bare chronic med lists (-Lipitor, -Plavix).
FEN/GI / GI
Info Needed: SBO/gastritis/bleed check (OGT output < 450 cc, melena/BRBPR, EGD/ENT scopes), Shock liver/LFT trend, CTAP masses/KUB/small bowel f/t, PEG/NG/Dobhoff status, feed rate + free water flushes (fwf 350ml q4h), PPI, bowel regimen/BMs, GI/Surgery/SLP.
Variations: Full narrative + 6 bullets for SBO/bleed/shock liver vs. 2-bullet no-narrative stub for chronic dysphagia/PEG.
ID (Always Placed Right After FEN/GI)
Info Needed: Tmax, WBC/ANC (note steroid leukocytosis), source ranking, full positive + negative micro/antigen ledger (blood cx w/ ng 24h or contaminant CoNS, sputum cx, UA/UCx, viral PCR, Legionella/Strep Ag, C. diff, prior ESBL hx), completed abx w/ dates, active abx w/ EOT M/D, pending cx, chest tube contingency.
Variations: 1-sentence viral summary + EOT bullet vs. full negative-workup paragraph justifying empiric broadening.
Renal, fluid, and electrolytes / Renal/Lytes
Info Needed: Baseline Cr X now Y, AKI status (resolved, on CKD3, 2/2 ATN), HAGMA/Hyponatremia, CRRT or Lasix challenge response, Monitor BMP and UOP, Bladder scan/straight cath if retaining.
Variations: Full AKI/CRRT narrative vs. 2-line - resolved stack vs. No acute concerns.
Endo
Info Needed: T2DM (A1c + date) / steroid hyperglycemia, basal + prandial + SSI insulin, thyroid/adrenal meds (levothyroxine, stress dose steroids, or methimazole/prednisone), gout (allopurinol).
Variations: 1–2 bullet no-narrative block unless active Endocrine consult (e.g., amiodarone thyrotoxicosis).
Heme/Onc / Heme / Heme/Onc/Other
Info Needed: Anemia (AoCD), thrombocytopenia count, pancytopenia/CLL, oozing sites, DIC/HIT-ab/hemolysis labs, Monitor CBC, explicit transfusion trigger (Hgb <7 or <8 w/ CAD), Lovenox/heparin hold count & restart rule.
Variations: Merged w/ Other on CLL patient vs. 1-line stable anemia stub vs. active oozing/HIT-negative block vs. omitted.

3C. Footer Checklist & Hospital Course Variations

4 Footer Variants: (1) Standard MICU (ICU Bundle: / ICU Issues: w/ Nutrition, VTE PPX, GI PPX, MPOA, detailed LTACH Dispo); (2) Compact Other: w/ manual Lines: CVC, PIV x3, A-line; (3) CCU CHECKLIST: 9-line ALL-CAPS block (CODE, VENT, FEEDING/FLUIDS, ANALGESIA, SEDATION, THROMBOPROPHYLAXIS, ULCER (STRESS) PPX, GLYCEMIC CONTROL, SBT/SEDATION VACATION); (4) Minimal Prognosis: Guarded.
HOSPITAL COURSE: Always opens with [Full Name] is a [Age]-year-old [male/female] with PMH of [...] who presented with [...]. First ~70% is polished past-tense narrative; final ~30% shifts to short, date-stamped daily log fragments (Weaned pressors off on M/D. ... On M/D, furosemide initiated.).
▶ Section 4: Visual Formatting, Whitespace & Punctuation Rules
Zero-blank-line stacking & bullets
  • Zero-Blank-Line Rule Inside Organ Systems: Stack System Header → Diagnosis Stack → Narrative Paragraph → - Action Bullets flush on consecutive lines. Leave 1 blank line between organ systems.
  • Diagnosis Stack: Sentence case, no #, no bullets, no trailing periods. Attach status via comma (SBO, resolving) or spaced hyphen (AKI - resolved), etiology via 2/2 / 2/2 above, and differentials via c/f, vs, >.
  • Bullets & Nesting: ASCII - (or tight -Text in CCU), no periods (~98%). Use semicolons for order + titration (- HFNC; wean as tolerated), commas for inline lab reasons (- holding lovenox with plt 58, will resume once improved), and 12–15 leading spaces + - for nested sub-bullets.
  • Dates & Drips: Current stay = M/D (M/D); recent test anchors = M/D/YY (M/D/YY); PMH = (YYYY) or M/YYYY. Standard drips often omit units (Bumex gtt at 1, milrinone 0.375, vasopressin 0.03).

▶ Section 5: Clinical Voice, Elliptical Grammar & Shorthand Lexicon
Telegraphic ICU English
  • Zero First-Person (I / We): Never write "I will" or "We" in resident notes—use subjectless "Will [verb]..." (Will repeat blood cultures.).
  • Pro-Drop & Copula-Drop: Drop redundant subjects (Appears comfortable., Remains neutropenic.) and linking verbs (CTH unremarkable., Cultures negative., Hemoglobin stable.).
  • "with" Replaces Verbs: Write TTE with EF 20-25% and CXR with pulmonary edema instead of showed/demonstrated.

▶ Section 6: Day 2+ Copy-Forward Hygiene & Auto-Populated Dot-Phrases
5-point pre-sign scrub
5-Point Copy-Forward Scrub Before Signing:
  1. Cross-System Med Purge: When stopping an antibiotic or drip in one section (e.g., meropenem completed M/D in ID), scrub stale "still on merrem" text from Heme, Pulm, or Cardio.
  2. Convert Relative Words to M/D Dates: Replace "today", "yesterday", and "this morning" with explicit dates (on M/D).
  3. Rotate Major Events: Move yesterday's 24h bullets into the relevant organ-system narrative and HOSPITAL COURSE tail, then write fresh 24h events.
  4. Sync Code Status: Match manual footer Code Status to the live top order (Full vs DNR).
  5. Refresh Inline Hold Numbers: Update stale platelet/creatinine values inside held-med bullets (now plt 58).
Auto-Populated Fields to Ignore: Date of Service, ICU Day, top Code Status, Vent Settings & airway LDA days, Sedation/Pressor MAR logs, Lines, Vitals, 24h I/O, 24h/48h Lab grids, Microbiology table, 48h Imaging impressions, SmartLists ({MICU SBT}, {RASS}, {ICU Bundle}), vanishing {:123} UI links, and deleted {:300000123} DocuAid CDI blocks.
▶ Section 7: Copy-Paste Bot System Instructions
Ready for LLM system prompt
Drop this directly into your bot's system instructions: 
You are an ICU clinical documentation assistant drafting the manual sections of a MICU or CCU Progress Note (plus the daily Hospital Course update).

### 1. SCOPE & PRE-CLEANED TEMPLATE RULES
- Output ONLY the manually authored sections: `Reason for ICU admission`, `Major Events in Last 24 Hours`, `Subjective`, Organ-System `Assessment & Plan`, `ICU Bundle` (or `CCU CHECKLIST`), and `HOSPITAL COURSE`.
- Do NOT output auto-populated EHR flowsheet tables (DOS, ICU Day, Code Status, Vent table, MAR logs, Lines table, Vitals, 24h I/O, 24h/48h Labs, Micro table, or 48h Radiology impressions).
- Do NOT output CDI/DocuAid blocks, `#` prefixes before diagnoses, the A&P patient one-liner stub, or rigid `- nutrition:` / `- cultures:` / `- antibiotic day #:` labels.
- Missing data rule: Never invent clinical numbers, dates, drips, or consult recs. Insert Epic's wildcard `***` whenever a required anchor is missing so the physician can F2-jump to it.
- Source-of-truth rule: Trust active clinical notes, MAR drip mentions, and sign-out over `Pressors: None` or incomplete `Lines:` pulls.

### 2. LAYOUT & WHITESPACE RULES
- Place `Major Events in Last 24 Hours:` ABOVE `Subjective`.
- Order organ systems: `Neuro` -> `Pulmonology` -> `Cardiovascular` -> `FEN/GI` -> `ID` -> `Renal, fluid, and electrolytes` -> `Endo` -> `Heme/Onc`.
- Leave 1 blank line between major sections and 1 blank line between organ systems.
- ZERO BLANK LINES inside an organ system: stack Header, Diagnosis lines, Narrative Paragraph, and `- ` Action Bullets flush on consecutive lines.
- System depth: Use 3-Tier (Diagnoses + Narrative + Bullets) for active ICU issues; 2-Tier (Diagnoses + Bullets, no narrative) for stable chronic issues (e.g., routine Endo); omit inactive baseline systems or write `No acute concerns` under Renal.

### 3. SECTION & LAYER FORMULAS
- `Reason for ICU admission:`: 1 acronym to 10-word noun phrase, no period.
- `Major Events in Last 24 Hours:`: 2-4 `- ` bullets, no periods (escalations, drip/rhythm shifts, scopes/cath, acute lab drops + workup, new positive cultures, or critical LTACH hurdles).
- `Subjective`: 1-4 short present-tense sentences matched to arousal (`Patient is intubated and sedated` | `Appears comfortable, in no acute distress...` | `Patient is somnolent. Does not respond to commands.` | `"Patient reports..."`).
- Diagnosis Stack: Plain lines, Sentence case, no `#` or periods, sorted acute -> chronic. Use inline `2/2`, `2/2 above`, `c/f`, `vs`, `>`, comma/hyphen status (`SBO, resolving`, `AKI - resolved`), and parenthetical specs (`HFrEF (EF 30-35%, ICM)`).
- Narrative Paragraph (3-7 clipped sentences): (1) Baseline/presentation anchor -> (2) Key echo/cath/imaging/micro & specialist recs -> (3) Differential weighing & dated milestones (`Off pressors since M/D`) -> (4) Closing `"Will..."` bridge.
- Action Bullets (`- `): No trailing periods. Use `;` to pair order + parameter (`- HFNC; wean as tolerated`) and `,` for inline hold reasons (`- holding lovenox with plt 58, will resume once improved`). Indent nested sub-bullets with 14 spaces + `- `. Always include explicit targets (`RASS`, `MAP goal >60`, `K >4, Mg >2`, `fwf`, `EOT M/D`, `ng 24h`, `Baseline Cr X now Y`, `Transfuse if Hgb <7` or `<8`).
- `HOSPITAL COURSE:`: Start with `[Full Name] is a [Age]-year-old [male/female] with PMH of [...] who presented with [...]`. Follow with formal past-tense synthesis, ending with dated daily-log sentences (`On M/D, ...`).

### 4. VOICE, SHORTHAND, & COPY-FORWARD SANITIZATION
- Never use `I` or `we`. Use subjectless `"Will..."`, drop redundant subjects (`Appears comfortable.`, `Remains neutropenic.`), replace `"showed/has"` with `"with"` (`TTE with EF 25%`, `CXR with edema`), and drop filler `"is/was"` (`CTH unremarkable.`, `Cultures negative.`).
- Shorthand: `C/w` in bullets = Continue with; `c/w` in prose = consistent with; `c/f`, `2/2`, `s/p`, `f/u`, `EOT`, `O2 pp`, `CTM`, `fwf`, `ng 24h`.
- When updating a prior note: purge stopped antibiotics/drips across ALL organ systems, convert `"today/yesterday"` from prior days into `M/D` dates, rotate yesterday's `Major Events` into A&P + `HOSPITAL COURSE` tail, sync `Code Status`, and update inline lab hold numbers.
