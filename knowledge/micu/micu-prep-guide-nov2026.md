## 3. UTMB MICU Logistics & Day-One Non-Negotiables

### Logistics (§8.1)
- **Location:** **Jennie Sealy 8B** (badge access required). Epic context: **`Jen 8B`** / **`Medicine Service`** (confirm via QGenda whether weeks 1–2 are MICU or HOS-N split).
- **Team Structure:** 2 IM Seniors + 1 IM Intern + PCCM Fellow + You.
  - **Admissions:** Fellow accepts admits; you evaluate the patient, write the H&P (`.MICUHP`), and place admission orders (`[314]`). Cap: 10 admits/resident.
- **Shift Hours:** Days **6:00 AM – 8:00 PM** | Nights **7:00 PM – 9:00 AM** (Sign-out at **6:00 AM / 6:00 PM** in the team room; 6 on / 3 off alternating).
- **Continuity Clinic:** 1 PM session/week (Wednesday PM on template); no Thursday didactics during MICU (or Week 4 only—confirm with chief).

### Day-One Survival Habits (§8.2)
1. **Always Report MAPs, Not Just SBP/DBP:** On ICU rounds, blood pressure is communicated as **MAP** (target $\ge 65\text{ mmHg}$ in sepsis/shock) alongside SBP/DBP ranges.
2. **Physically Check the IV Pumps in the Room:** Epic MAR drip rates frequently lag behind nurse titration by 15–30 minutes. Walk into every room on pre-rounds and write down the **actual running rate on the pump** for every pressor, inotrope, and sedative.
3. **Know Every Vent Setting Cold:** Mode (e.g., PC/AC, VC/AC, PRVC, PS), FiO2, PEEP, set RR vs. actual patient RR, Tidal Volume ($V_t$ in mL and mL/kg IBW), Peak Pressure ($P_{peak}$), and Plateau Pressure ($P_{plat}$, target $< 30\text{ cmH}_2\text{O}$).
4. **Note Expectations:**
   - **Night shift:** Writes the full Progress Note (cosigned).
   - **Day shift:** Writes a brief interval update (**`.MICUBRIEF`**).
   - **Admissions:** Use **`.MICUHP`**.
   - **Standard Template:** **`MICU PROGRESS NOTE`** includes DocuAid auto-text blocks (shock, anemia, coagulopathy, malnutrition)—every `***` wildcard must be completed before signing.
   - Have notes mostly drafted **before rounds**; every abnormal lab/finding needs a line in the A/P, and be ready to explain *"why?"* for every active order.
5. **Phone Numbers to Grab at 6:00 AM:** MICU Fellow, Cardiology consult, GI consult. Know whether you are carrying the code pager (be ready to lead/perform compressions).
6. **Procedures — Do Every One Offered & Log Same Day in NI:**
   - MICU is your primary rotation to hit FM graduation minimums: **Central Line (3)**, **Arterial Puncture/Line (3)**, **Intubation (3)**, **ABG Interp (5)**, **CXR Interp (25)**, **NG Tube (3)**, **Paracentesis (3)**, **Thoracentesis (3)**.
   - **Obtain informed consent yourself** (you are explicitly graded on indications, contraindications, complications, consent, technique, post-care, and interpretation).

### B. Pre-Rounding Chart Check Path (5–7 Min Per Patient)
1. **Summary Tab -> ICU Quick Overview / MICU Rounding Report:**
   - Wrench this tab into your sidebar. Gives a single-screen view of vitals, vent trends, drips, lines, and micro.
2. **Flowsheets Tab:**
   - **Vitals:** Q1H/Q2H vitals, Tmax, and **MAP range** over the last 24h.
   - **Respiratory / Ventilator:** Mode, FiO2, PEEP, RR (set/actual), $V_t$, $P_{peak}$, $P_{plat}$, RSBI, SAT/SBT results.
   - **Intake / Output (I/O):** 24h net balance, shift balance, cumulative ICU fluid balance, and **hourly urine output** (target $\ge 0.5\text{ mL/kg/h}$; flag oliguria early).
3. **MAR Tab -> Continuous Infusions:**
   - Check active pressors, sedation, insulin, heparin, diuretic drips, and scheduled antibiotics (day # of therapy). Cross-check against bedside pumps.
4. **LDA (Lines, Drains, Airways) Tab:**
   - **Airway:** ETT size (e.g., 7.5 or 8.0), depth at teeth/lip (cm), day # of intubation.
   - **Vascular Access:** CVC site/lumen count & placement date (evaluate daily for removal), arterial line site, PIVs.
   - **Tubes/Drains:** Foley (check daily necessity), NG/OG/Dobhoff (confirm CXR placement note before feeding/meds), chest tubes (suction vs. water seal, output).
5. **Results Review & Micro Summary (90 Days):**
   - Check morning BMP, Mg, Phos, CBC, Coags, Lactate, ABG/VBG, portable CXR, and pending blood/urine/respiratory cultures.

## 5. High-Yield UTMB MICU Clinical Protocols (§8.4–8.5)

### A. Sepsis & Septic Shock (UTMB Specifics)
- **Fluids:** **30 mL/kg** crystalloid bolus (LR or NS; **avoid LR if $K > 5.0$**). Consider **Albumin 5% 25 g** if serum albumin $< 3.0\text{ g/dL}$.
- **Workup:** Lactate panel with automatic **2-hour repeat if $> 2.0\text{ mmol/L}$**; **2 sets of blood cultures** prior to antibiotics (do not delay abx $> 45\text{ min}$).
- **Pressors:** **Norepinephrine** first-line (start $0.05\text{ mcg/kg/min}$, titrate to **MAP $\ge 65\text{ mmHg}$**). Add **Vasopressin** ($0.03\text{–}0.04\text{ units/min}$ fixed rate, non-titratable) when norepi requirements climb. Consider hydrocortisone $200\text{ mg/day}$ ($50\text{ mg IV q6h}$) for refractory shock.
- **UTMB Empiric Antibiotic Regimens by Source:**
  - **Unknown Source:** Vancomycin + Cefepime + Metronidazole
  - **Intra-Abdominal:** Vancomycin + Piperacillin-Tazobactam (Zosyn)
  - **Critically Ill / MDR Risk:** Vancomycin + Meropenem

### B. Vasopressor, Inotrope & Sedation Quick-Reference Table

| Drug | Class / Role | UTMB Dosing Range | Key Pearls & Caveats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Norepinephrine (Levophed)** | First-line pressor ($\alpha_1 > \beta_1$) | `0.05 – 1.5 mcg/kg/min` | First choice in septic, cardiogenic (with inotrope), and hypovolemic shock. Target MAP $\ge 65$. |
| **Vasopressin** | Second-line ($V_1$ receptor) | `0.03 – 0.04 units/min` | **Fixed dose** (not titrated). Added to spare norepi; pure vasoconstrictor. |
| **Epinephrine** | Pressor + Inotrope ($\alpha_1, \beta_1, \beta_2$) | `0.01 – 0.5+ mcg/kg/min` | First-line in anaphylaxis; second/third-line in septic shock or bradycardic/low-CO shock. Raises lactate via $\beta_2$ glycolysis. |
| **Phenylephrine (Neo-Synephrine)** | Pure $\alpha_1$ agonist | `0.5 – 6 mcg/kg/min` | Useful in vagal/neurogenic hypotension or severe tachycardia/AF-RVR where $\beta_1$ stimulation is harmful. Can drop CO via reflex bradycardia. |
| **Dopamine** | Dose-dependent ($D_1, \beta_1, \alpha_1$) | `2.5 – 20 mcg/kg/min` | **Central line only.** High arrhythmia risk (AF/VT); rarely preferred over norepi. |
| **Dobutamine** | Inotrope ($\beta_1 > \beta_2$) | `2.5 – 20 mcg/kg/min` | For low cardiac output / cardiogenic shock. Causes peripheral vasodilation (may drop BP—often paired with norepi). |
| **Fentanyl** | Analgesic (Opioid) | `25 – 200 mcg/h` | **Analgesia-first ICU protocol.** Titrate to CPOT / RASS `-2 to 0` before adding sedatives. |
| **Propofol** | Sedative-hypnotic (GABA) | `5 – 50 mcg/kg/min` | Fast onset/offset for daily SAT; causes hypotension, bradycardia, hypertriglyceridemia (check TG after 48h). |
| **Dexmedetomidine (Precedex)** | $\alpha_2$ agonist (non-respiratory) | `0.2 – 1.5 mcg/kg/h` | Ideal for agitated delirium or extubation bridge (does not suppress respiratory drive). Watch for bradycardia & hypotension. |

### C. Mechanical Ventilation & ARDS
- **UTMB Default Initial Setup:** **Pressure Control / Assist-Control (PC/AC)** or Volume Control (AC/VC), initial **FiO2 100%**, **PEEP 5 cmH2O**, **RR 12–20/min** (titrate quickly down on FiO2 using SpO2 92–96% or ARDSnet goals 88–95%).
- **ARDS Lung-Protective Ventilation:** Tidal volume **$6\text{ mL/kg}$ Ideal Body Weight (IBW)** (range $4\text{–}8\text{ mL/kg}$), **Plateau pressure ($P_{plat}$) $\le 30\text{ cmH}_2\text{O}$**, permissive hypercapnia (keep $\text{pH} \ge 7.20\text{–}7.25$). Prone positioning $\ge 16\text{ h/day}$ if $P_aO_2/F_iO_2 < 150$.
- **Daily Liberation Protocol (ABCDEF Bundle):**
  - **SAT (Spontaneous Awakening Trial):** Daily sedation interruption at **06:00 AM** starting 24–48h post-intubation (unless active seizures, alcohol withdrawal, paralytics, escalating pressors, or ICP crisis).
  - **SBT (Spontaneous Breathing Trial):** Daily **QAM** on Pressure Support ($5\text{–}8\text{ cmH}_2\text{O}$ / PEEP 5). Check RSBI ($RR / V_t(L) < 105$), mental status, cuff leak, and secretions.
  - **BiPAP (NIV):** First-line for acute hypercapnic COPD exacerbation and cardiogenic pulmonary edema.

### D. Daily ICU Prophylaxis Checklist (`FAST HUGS BID`)
- **VTE Prophylaxis (Padua $\ge 4$):**
  - Standard: **Enoxaparin 40 mg SC daily at 17:00**
  - $\text{CrCl} < 30\text{ mL/min}$: **Enoxaparin 30 mg SC daily**
  - $\text{CrCl} < 10\text{ mL/min}$ / ESRD: **Unfractionated Heparin 5,000 units SC q8–12h**
  - $\text{BMI} > 40\text{ kg/m}^2$: **Enoxaparin 40 mg SC BID**
- **Stress Ulcer Prophylaxis (SUP):**
  - **Indications:** Mechanical ventilation $\ge 48\text{ h}$, coagulopathy (platelets $< 50	ext{k}$, INR $> 1.5$, PTT $> 2\times$), GI bleed history, or $\ge 2$ minor criteria (sepsis, ICU LOS $> 7\text{d}$, occult bleed, high-dose steroids).
  - **Agents:** **Famotidine 20 mg IV/PO BID** (avoid if platelets $< 150	ext{k}$ per UTMB note) or **Pantoprazole 40 mg IV/PO daily**.
- **Other Daily Bundle Items:** HOB elevated $30^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$, oral care, glycemic control ($140\text{–}180\text{ mg/dL}$), daily assessment to remove Foley & central lines.

### E. Admission & Transfer Criteria (IHOP 09.01.23)
- **MICU Admission Triggers:** Mechanical ventilation or imminent airway compromise; invasive hemodynamic monitoring (A-line/PA catheter); titratable vasoactive or antiarrhythmic drips (any continuous drip other than heparin); unstable arrhythmia; massive UGIB/transfusion; $> 3$ doses of Naloxone (Narcan); worsening septic shock.
- **Who Writes Orders:** Pulm-Crit (MICU) or Cardiology (CCU) owns all orders; only Nephrology (dialysis/CRRT) and Anesthesia (pre-op) may write orders without primary team countersignature.
- **Transfer-Out Criteria:** Admitting acute issue resolved, liberated from mechanical ventilation, off invasive hemodynamic monitoring, off titratable drips, code status clarified. *(Note: Suicide attempt / intentional overdose patients **must** be evaluated and cleared by Psychiatry before floor transfer).*

## 6. Systems-Based MICU Rounding Presentation Template

Use this exact structure on morning rounds so nothing falls through the cracks:

1. **One-Liner & Overnight Events:** *"Mr. X is a 64yo M with COPD, HFrEF (EF 30%), ICU Day 3 for septic shock secondary to severe CAP, intubated on Day 1. Overnight: [events, PRNs, lines placed, code status]."*
2. **Vitals & Hemodynamics:** Tmax/Tcurrent, HR range/rhythm, **MAP range** (and SBP/DBP), current pressor(s) and **exact bedside pump rates** (e.g., *"Norepi at 0.08 mcg/kg/min, down from 0.15 yesterday"*).
3. **Neuro / Pain / Sedation:** Current RASS (vs. target), CPOT/pain score, confusion/CAM-ICU, active infusions (Fentanyl, Propofol, Precedex), SAT result at 06:00.
4. **Respiratory / Vent:** Vent mode, FiO2, PEEP, set RR / actual RR, $V_t$, $P_{peak} / P_{plat}$, ABG ($\text{pH} / P_aCO_2 / P_aO_2 / HCO_3$), SBT performance, secretions, portable CXR findings.
5. **Cardiovascular:** Exam, rhythm/tele, troponin/BNP trends, pressor weaning plan, fluid responsiveness.
6. **GI / Nutrition:** Exam, bowel sounds/last BM, tube feeds (type, rate vs. goal) or NPO reason, LFTs, stress ulcer prophylaxis (Famotidine vs. PPI).
7. **Renal / Fluid Balance / Electrolytes:** **24h Intake / Output, net shift & cumulative balance**, **UOP in mL/kg/h**, Cr/BUN trend (baseline Cr), K / Mg / Phos / Ca repletion, acid-base status, CRRT/HD settings per Nephrology.
8. **Infectious Disease:** Tmax, WBC trend, procalcitonin/lactate, **all active antibiotics with Day #** (e.g., *"Vanc + Cefepime Day 3/7"*), blood/urine/sputum culture updates, MRSA nares.
9. **Heme / Prophylaxis:** Hgb/Plt/INR trends, VTE prophylaxis (Enoxaparin vs. SC Heparin vs. SCDs if bleeding).
10. **Endocrine:** Blood glucose range over last 24h, insulin drip or sliding-scale/basal requirements.
11. **Lines, Drains, Airways (LDA) & Dispo:** ETT depth at teeth, Central Line site & Day #, A-line site & Day #, Foley necessity, family update / goals of care.
