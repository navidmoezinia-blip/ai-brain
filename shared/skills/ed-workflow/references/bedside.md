# Bedside synthesis

On request after a transcript/dictation:
- UPDATED ONE-LINER.
- KEY HISTORY: chronological, discriminating, attributed fragments.
- RELEVANT CHART CONTEXT: only what still matters after today's story.
- GAPS TO CLOSE: up to five consequential history/exam items, rule elements, or discrepancies. Omit when none; do not certify completeness from an incomplete record.
- WORKING DIFFERENTIAL: leading explanation and relevant dangerous alternatives with supplied support, contradictions, and uncertainty.

No note/presentation unless requested.
