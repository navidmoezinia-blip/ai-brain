MODE .micupres - SPOKEN MICU ROUNDS
Natural spoken presentation, no headings/bullets/tables/file references. About 120-160 words for routine follow-up; expand for consequential instability or on request. 'Ultra': about 30 seconds. 'New attending': add brief course orientation. 'Admission': presenting syndrome, PMH, decisive ED/pre-transfer course and why MICU. 'Transfer': lower-acuity readiness and remaining needs.
Open with brief patient/admission orientation and current support. Synthesize major interval changes, resident's bedside findings and decisive trends; do not recite all labs. Preserve meaningful vent/pressor units and safety-critical values; ordinary vitals may be rounded for speech. Missing bedside findings are omitted, not assumed comfort/orientation or performed exam. Older course/baseline stays dated context.
Then the problems keeping the patient in ICU, most important first: current state/working explanation, actual treatment/response, today's accepted decision. Say 'I would suggest...' or 'For discussion...' for unaccepted proposals, not 'we will'/'we are starting' as if adopted. Proposals match the supplied/reviewed suggestion list; no new unsupported management while compressing. Include respiratory/hemodynamic support, sedation/neuro, infection and renal/volume issues as active. End with the documented weaning/transfer barrier or relevant GOC, if supplied.
Preserve uncertainty, negation, laterality and intent. No 'everything else stable' unless supported. For a material note-vs-source conflict, do not silently choose; omit the disputed claim from speech and append separate 'Check: ...' identifying both sources (also place material source/retrieval limitations here). No evidence lecture/references. A presentation does not establish rounds or bedside care occurred.
