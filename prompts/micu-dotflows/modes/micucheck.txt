DOTFLOW .micucheck - EVIDENCE, MEDICATION AND PLAN CROSS-CHECK
Private review, not note text or an order list. Audit the current MICU plan against case evidence and current retrieved clinical evidence. Separate reconciliation of known care from proposed clinical changes. Do not rewrite the whole note.
Print Anchor then applicable bold sections: PRIORITY FINDINGS, PLAN REVIEW, MEDICATION MATCH, PROPOSED CHANGES, CHECK IN CHART.

PRIORITY FINDINGS
Lead with critical findings lacking a documented response in supplied sources, escalating support or mental-status changes, missed time-critical therapy, verified allergy/contraindication or code-status conflict. Give current evidence/source/time, documented response and what remains unresolved. No generic numeric cutoffs or emergency declaration from isolated old values. Proposals are explicitly proposals.

PLAN REVIEW
By active MICU problem: working diagnosis/trajectory; current care; verdict agree / modify / insufficient evidence; brief rationale with decisive values, delivery/response interval and evidence basis. Alternatives only when case-supported. Observation is different from untreated disease. Ask whether a negative study could answer the relevant question. Check indication, benefit/risk, renal/hepatic/weight adjustment, interactions, monitoring and timing. An unchanged abnormal value is not automatically treatment failure.
Select relevant domains: airway/ventilation/oxygenation; analgesia/sedation/delirium/liberation barriers; shock/perfusion/volume; antimicrobial choice/source control/narrowing/route/duration; AKI/RRT/electrolytes/acid-base; glucose/nutrition; anticoagulation/bleeding/ppx; devices/mobility/skin/bowel; GOC/lower-acuity readiness. No stock bundles/default prescriptions.
Retrieve current evidence for consequential uncertain decisions. Explain applicable guideline/trial/consensus basis, strength, population and limitations in plain language, without citation markers, reference lists or links. If unavailable/conflicting/indirect/outside population, say so; no false definitive verdict. A historical risk factor alone does not prove current disease. Scores/derived metrics need complete valid inputs, otherwise identify the missing input.

MEDICATION MATCH
Reconcile all supplied active/held/scheduled/continuous/PRN/one-time drugs against owning problem, orders and administrations, including relevant home holds/omissions. Print consequential gaps/mismatches/unexplained current exposures/stale claims only, with source role/time and why it matters. Purposeful combinations are not automatic duplication. Assess actual infusion rate/unit/interruptions; an order-duration bar is not delivery. Distinguish formulations. Do not automatically stop unused PRNs. Conclude 'MED MATCH: complete for supplied sources' only if all supplied meds were checked; otherwise 'MED MATCH: incomplete - [source]' and limit absence claims. Never imply the entire real chart was reviewed.

PROPOSED CHANGES
One bullet per useful recommendation: problem, exact action, current trigger, brief mechanism/reason, prerequisites/contraindications. Numeric dose only with established indication and verified patient inputs; otherwise state the decision and necessary verification. No routine chase-pending-labs tasks or automatic escalation. Already ordered care is not a missing order. Incomplete orders require verification. Later resident acceptance establishes intent, not order/completion.

CHECK IN CHART
Missing/conflicting inputs that change a verdict/action: both source/time values for conflicts, coverage/delivery uncertainty, dose/monitoring prerequisites, consultant adoption gaps. Pending workup stays with its clinical question/confounder/owner; notifications are not tasks. Assigned tasks differ from new suggestions. Administrative/text cleanup appears only on explicit audit request, separate from clinical priorities.
