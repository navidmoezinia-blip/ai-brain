SLOT CONTENT
Day counts need verified dates/counting convention. CC: 1-2 words. HPI continues native demographic prefix: baseline/PMH, admission reason, supported historian/barrier, symptom evolution, collateral/med-rec or dated pre-transfer course, immediate status and ICU trigger. Length follows complexity. Past tense for course; clipped current status. Historical wishes are not current code orders. Label OSH vs receiving-ED care; N/A only if no ED episode confirmed. Include unpulled pertinent OSH imaging/TTE/RHC with timing. Gas type/time/support must match; never relabel VBG as ABG. ROS inability needs a supported barrier. Current resident exam only: no cross-slot copying, normal defaults or assumed rectal deferral. Flag conflicting arousal/orientation separately.

A/P STRUCTURE
Each system: native support lines, diagnoses acute to chronic, compact reasoning, actions. Link diagnosis/uncertainty, decisive findings, baseline/response. Adopted/ordered/given status stays explicit; AI proposals after checklist in SUGGESTIONS. No numbered hospitalist problems, PLAN labels, forced italics or hospitalist appendices/footer.
Neuro: mentation, sedation/analgesia, documented goals and neuro/psych medication decisions.
Resp: failure/airway indication, support/settings/units, gas/imaging, documented weaning/SAT/SBT decision and barrier.
Cardiovascular: shock/HF/rhythm reasoning, perfusion, support and volume response, echo/cath, adopted cardiac/antithrombotic decisions. Keep template pressor label but explicitly state each agent's ACTUAL unit; do not convert all infusions to mcg/kg/min or confuse airway pressure with blood-pressure MAP.
FEN/GI: GI/hepatic/surgical problem, feeds/rate/tolerance, bowel and ulcer prophylaxis decisions.
ID: infection certainty/source, host factors, culture timing/status, antimicrobial regimen and plan-derived duration, source control and specialist recs/adoption.
Renal: baseline/current function, interval-specific UOP/I&O, electrolyte/acid-base issue, response and renal support. A partial interval is not a complete 24h balance.
Endo: glucose and prescribed vs administered insulin, thyroid/steroid issues; no example-derived targets/rates.
Other: Heme/Onc/systemic problems, bleeding/thrombosis/ppx, device type/site/date/need, supported surrogate relationship/GOC/prognosis. External urinary device is not an indwelling catheter.
