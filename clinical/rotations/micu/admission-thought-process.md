# MICU admission thought process

Source: session with Claude, 2026-11-16. Reference: Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2026 (SCCM/ESICM), update of 2021.
https://www.sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/surviving-sepsis-campaign-international-guidelines-for-management-of-sepsis-and-septic-shock-2026

## First question, every admission
- Why can't this patient be on the floor? Answer this before anything else.

## Consult call: pull these before hanging up
- What organ is failing, and how fast
- What has been done so far (fluids, pressors, O2, antibiotics, access)
- Vitals with trend, MAP, last gas / lactate
- Code status, and whether family has been reached

## Ask the ED doc (not in the chart)
- What source are they betting on
- How did the patient actually respond to fluids
- Did they hold back on fluids on purpose (HF, bad lungs), and why
- Did cultures go before antibiotics; which antibiotic
- Does the patient look sick to them, or is it just the lactate

## Septic shock plan
- MAP < 65 after 30 cc/kg: start norepinephrine and discuss central line; give further fluid only if dynamic reassessment shows fluid responsiveness and overload risk is acceptable
- Lactate > 4: recheck in 2 hours
- Source control early: imaging or surgery moving, antibiotics don't drain pus
- SSC 2026 changes to note:
  - No empiric anaerobic coverage unless anaerobic risk factors present (conditional, very low certainty)
  - Active fluid removal after resuscitation to prevent fluid overload
