**Browser automation lesson (Aug 24):** coordinate-based click-then-paste into the Qualified Health/Doximity Instructions textareas silently failed twice this session (stale content, activeElement was BODY) even though the clipboard held the correct text — click coordinates don't reliably hit the right element across page layouts/scales. **Previsit rebuild (Aug 22, 2026).** The weak link he named is not knowing what questions to ask, under a ~60-second window between hearing the chief complaint and walking in. Stage 1 was rebuilt as PREVISIT in both the prompt and the skill: ASK comes first (8-12 spoken lines, each tagged with the diagnosis it discriminates; untaggable lines get cut), then KILLERS, CHART (omitted entirely when no chart was pasted), EXAM, STOP THE INTERVIEW IF. No bracket-filling — one messy paste, and it produces a guide from a bare complaint if that's all he has.

**C:\Users\nav\ed-hpi-templates\ED-Previsit-Launchers.md** is the speed layer: a paste-once ENGINE plus a one-line launcher per problem carrying that problem's can't-miss seed. Covers common complaints, host-defined patients (dialysis, oncology, transplant, sickle cell, pregnant/postpartum, device, nonverbal), and in-progress emergencies (shock, crash airway, arrest, peri-intubation, transfusion reaction, deterioration of a boarded patient, surge). Seeds are a floor, not a ceiling. `ED-HPI-Templates.md` gained HPI-0, the one-paste version of HPI-1.

**History reconciliation (Aug 22, 2026).** The CHART block was replaced by **PRIORS** (max 3, usually zero) under one routing rule: *the chart is reliable for dated events and numbers, the patient is reliable for current state.* Current-state facts can only become an ASK line; dated/numeric facts he can't get from the patient can only become a PRIORS line; nothing appears in both; anything fitting neither is silent. PMH/PSHx/SocHx/FHx/meds/allergies got **no sections of their own** — deliberately, since a block empty 4 patients in 5 teaches skimming. ASK tags now carry the generating fact (`[mesenteric ischemia — AF]`), history-derived lines cap at 3 of 12, `[dispo]` at 2 with no floor. History **promotes killers, never demotes**. Guard: never print the absence of history (no NKDA, no "family history noncontributory") — absent means nobody asked, and printing it can falsely reassure. Disposition/social questions are framed as *order-timing* questions (PT/OT, records, transport have hours of latency; EMS and family are perishable collateral).
