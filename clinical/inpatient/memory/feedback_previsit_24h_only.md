Pre-visit guide (inpatient-rounding "p1") = last 24 hours only. Interval events, tasks, questions, and exam come from what changed in the 24h window. Don't carry over admission-long items (old consults, old imaging, chronic med-optimization ideas like HCQ dosing, PPI step-down, isolation cleanup). Those belong in the full note, appendices, or SUGGESTIONS.

**Why:** 10/31/2026 he got a p1 that was far too long because it pulled in everything from admission; he supplies the full chart for the note, not for the guide.

His framing: daily sections (interval events, interval labs, the whole pre-visit guide) are the delta vs his LAST PROGRESS NOTE; cumulative history lives in the A/P running record and Appendices A-D. Rule added to the inpatient-rounding SKILL.md R3 "SCOPE" line 10/31/2026. Also: check the pasted prior note is actually the latest one (10/31 he fed an old note by mistake).

**How to apply:** For p1, filter every item by "did this happen or change in the 24h before the anchor?" Older unresolved items only if they directly drive today's decision. Keep the guide short. Related: [[feedback-build-exactly-as-specified]]
